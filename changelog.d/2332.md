### Fixed

- **Stopping a bot task right after wmux starts stops its WSL worker too.**
  A stop that came before wmux knew its workspaces skipped every WSL
  terminal, so the task's WSL worker kept running and held an agent slot.
  wmux now stops a WSL worker it can identify at once, waits for the
  workspace list for the others, and reports the stop as partial until
  then. (#2332)
