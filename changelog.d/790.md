### Fixed

- **Scattered wrong glyphs no longer survive a Korean-heavy output burst.** The
  shared WebGL glyph atlas guard spotted a page merge only by the pool
  *shrinking*, but a merge is a net -2 and the very burst that causes it
  re-allocates those pages within the guard's 2s poll — so the count looked
  unchanged, the repair never ran, and the corruption stayed until a new pane
  was opened. The guard now recognises a merge by page identity, which survives
  the regrowth, and keeps that comparison alive even while its preventive path
  is firing on every poll. The repair log now says which signal fired, so the
  two cases are distinguishable in the field. (#790)
