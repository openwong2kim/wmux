### Added

- **Native TLS for `wmux web`.** Use `--tls-cert <path>` with `--tls-key <path>` to terminate HTTPS directly when exposing panes without Tailscale. (#775)

### Security

- **Fail-closed HTTPS restore.** Native TLS stores certificate/key paths rather than PEM bytes, validates material before replacing a live listener, leaves no plaintext listener if persisted TLS material becomes unavailable, and rotates the bearer token before an explicit HTTPS-to-HTTP downgrade. (#775)
