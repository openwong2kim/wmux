### Security

- **Restricted raw browser attachment metadata.** `browser.cdp.info` now returns `cdpPort` and `shellUrl` only to operator, validated pinned, or recognized first-party wire callers; approved third-party and legacy callers retain target metadata without the raw attach primitive. Browser tools with an RPC equivalent continue through the workspace-scoped, lease-covered fallback, while operations requiring a direct Playwright page report that attachment is unavailable. (#844)
