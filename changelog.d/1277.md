### Fixed

- **Double-clicking a pane divider evens out the split again** (#1233, #1277): a real double-click on the divider between two panes collapsed one of them instead of splitting their space evenly. It now gives the pair 50/50 in either direction, and only the pair at that divider changes in nested or three-plus splits. A snap to a layout or a double-click no longer gets overwritten a moment later by the layout the panes had before.
- **Unstashed panes come back with a proper split** (#1277): a pane brought back from the stash, including one a layout snap put there, now always lands in a split whose sizes add up to 100%, instead of carrying over its share of the old, larger row into the saved session.
