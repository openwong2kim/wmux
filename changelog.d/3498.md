### Added

- **Switch accounts by quota now works for WSL panes.** When the account a WSL launch is bound to is out of quota, wmux moves the launch to another account in the same group. It judges each account only by the usage its WSL panes in that distro reported for the same Linux user, so a Windows sign-in never counts for a WSL launch, and the reverse. An account whose last WSL turn failed on its sign-in is not picked. wmux never reads files inside the distro and never starts a stopped distro to decide. (#3498)
