### Added

- **Fan-out tasks can declare what they write and what they wait for.**
  `fanout_start` takes `files` (per-task repo-relative globs; overlapping
  scopes between tasks of one fan-out are refused before anything spawns, and
  each worker is told its scope) and `depends_on` (per-task indices that must
  finish first; cycles and out-of-range indices are refused). A dependent
  task's worktree is created only once every dependency reaches
  `review_requested` or `completed`, from a freshly fetched origin commit, and
  its prompt names the dependency branches to merge if they are not on origin
  yet. Before, every task of a fan-out started at once with nothing keeping two
  workers off the same files.
