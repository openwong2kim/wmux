### Added

- **Groundwork for reading chat v2 conversations on the phone.** The phone
  routes can now serve a conversation the daemon runs itself (chat v2) as a
  read-only Chat view with its approvals, Stop and the cancel outcome, and the
  daemon can hand such a conversation over to the pane's terminal. Nothing
  changes in the app yet: no conversation runs this way until the chat v2
  agent driver is switched on.
