### Changed

- **Git lives in the sidebar now.** The tools panel's Git tab moved to a
  collapsible section at the foot of the sidebar, for the repo behind your
  active pane: the current branch (ahead/behind, uncommitted changes with
  Diff, the PR and its CI state), pull requests with their comments, and one
  row per worktree that names the workspaces on it. Open, Diff, Merge and
  Remove work as before. The section never takes more than 45% of the
  sidebar, drags to resize, and remembers its height and whether it is
  folded; folded, it stops reading git. The tools panel keeps the
  Orchestrator (and Channels if you turned them on), and its titlebar dot
  no longer counts dirty worktrees.

### Fixed

- **Merge session labels showed raw keys.** Merge, Land, Discard and the
  merge phases read `git.merge` and the like instead of words.
