### Fixed

- **A busy Claude pane no longer has its whole output ring re-parsed every
  10 seconds.** StallWatch's screen read and a headless box's question check
  parsed a Claude pane's whole ring (up to 8 MiB) on every cycle, because the
  fast tail read always misses on Claude's cell-diff screen. On a box this kept
  the daemon over its 500 MB warning. Both now read the ring's screen
  checkpoint and the bytes after it, and keep that checkpoint close to the
  output. Measured on one pane with a full 8 MiB ring: about 17 MB parsed per
  cycle before, about 0.3 MB after. They also read cells the ring had lost,
  so the question check says "clear" where it said "unknown" before. (#3523)

### Added

- **A headless box prunes merged worktrees by itself.** Every hour, and two
  minutes after a headless start ends, the box daemon removes the kept
  worktree of an ended start when the worktree is clean and its commits are
  in origin's default branch (fetched first) or are the head of a merged pull
  request (checked with `gh`). Unpushed work, work that is pushed but not
  merged, and uncommitted changes stay. One log line gives how many worktrees
  stay and how large they are. `wmux daemon headless prune` and its
  `--dry-run` work as before. (#3523)
