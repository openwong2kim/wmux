### Fixed

- **The "Link with a pane on another PC" dialog works from the keyboard.** The PC picker and the pane list are now one Tab stop each. In the PC picker, arrow keys move between PCs and select them, wrapping at the ends, and Home/End jump to the first and last PC. In the pane list, Up/Down and Home/End move between panes and Enter or Space picks one. Before, every PC and pane was its own Tab stop and arrow keys did nothing. (#1971)
