### Added

- **Alt+Up / Alt+Down can be switched off.** wmux always took these two keys
  to cycle workspaces, so TUI apps that bind them — Codex, Crush and other
  CLI agents — never received them, and the combos could not be reassigned
  either. They are now listed in Settings → Shortcuts as "Previous workspace"
  and "Next workspace", each with its own toggle. Turning one off sends the key
  to the terminal instead and frees the combo for a custom keybinding.
  Workspaces stay reachable without them: the prefix bindings still cycle
  previous / next, and Ctrl+1–9 (⌘1–9 on macOS) jumps straight to one (#1456).
