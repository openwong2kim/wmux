### Fixed

- **Terminal history comes back at the sizes it was written at.** When a pane reattaches, is revealed or is restored after its session ended, output from before a resize now reflows the way it did on screen. Before, it re-wrapped at the current width and inline TUI redraws landed on the wrong rows. Busy panes and full-screen apps that do not set scroll regions are now restored from a compact snapshot. Before, the pane got up to 8 MB of raw output and stayed blank while it was re-parsed. (#1825)
