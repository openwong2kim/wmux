### Fixed

- **An approval refused by policy no longer re-opens raw typing.** When the orchestrator's `approval_press` was refused because a worker's autonomy or approval pressing was off, typing into that pane used to be allowed again for ten minutes. The refusal now points the orchestrator to `deck_ask_decision`, and the pane stays closed to its keystrokes for a while, with or without an approval record. Ctrl+C and Escape still stop the worker, and you can always type into it yourself. Structured questions from a worker are now answered through `approval_press` with the chosen option, so workspace autonomy applies to them too. (#1553)
