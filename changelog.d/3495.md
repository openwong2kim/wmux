### Fixed

- **WSL pane usage now counts for its account.** A WSL pane on a registered Claude account now reports its 5-hour and weekly usage under that account, the way a Windows pane does. Before, the distro's folder path matched no account, and the sample was dropped. (#3495)
- **agy launches in WSL panes are no longer held by the agy account gate.** wmux switches agy accounts through the Windows sign-in, which agy in WSL does not use, so wmux no longer switches accounts for an agy launch in WSL or holds it. (#3495)
