### Added

- **Inline images in terminal panes.** Images that terminal programs print with sixel or the iTerm2 image protocol (OSC 1337) now draw inside the pane on the desktop, and the terminal reports sixel support to apps that ask. Each pane keeps at most 64 MB of images; older ones become a placeholder. Turn it off under Settings › Terminal › Inline images. Images do not survive a reattach that restores the screen from the daemon snapshot. Kitty graphics are not supported. (#1641)
