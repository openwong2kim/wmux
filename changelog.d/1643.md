### Added

- **Inline images in terminal panes.** Images that terminal programs print with sixel or the iTerm2 image protocol (OSC 1337) now draw inside the pane on the desktop, and the terminal reports sixel support to apps that ask. Panes split a 256 MB image budget (between 8 and 64 MB each); older ones become a placeholder. Turn it off under Settings › Terminal › Inline images. Known limitation: after the app reconnects to a session whose recent output is 256 KB or more, the screen is restored from a text-only snapshot and the images in it are not redrawn. Kitty graphics are not supported. (#1641)
