### Added

- **Answer Moa's own permission prompt without the terminal.** When Moa stops on its own "Do you want to proceed?" prompt, the Moa chat now shows what it wants to run with the prompt's choices, and a paired phone gets the same card in its approvals inbox. Before, the chat only said Moa was waiting and the phone could not answer at all: you had to switch to Moa's terminal. The answer goes through the same checks as any other prompt card (it must still be the prompt on screen, one answer only, not in the first moment the card appears), and Moa's own automatic approvals never press it. When the card cannot be tied to the exact command, it offers Answer in terminal only. (#1772)
