### Fixed

- A link that opens a new tab in a workspace's wmux Chrome (a `target=_blank` link, `window.open`, a popup) now opens a tab of that workspace. The phone's browser list and `browser_tabs` show it, and it can be driven and streamed like any other. Before, the new tab belonged to no workspace, so the phone kept showing the old tab.

### Added

- Phone contract v1.7: a tap that opens a new tab answers with `opened: {surfaceId}`.
