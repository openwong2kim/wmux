### Changed

- **Idle workspaces with a browser or editor tab now free their terminals too.** "Unload idle hidden workspaces" used to skip any workspace that also held a browser, editor or diff tab, so its terminals stayed loaded for as long as the app ran. Such a workspace is now unloaded after the same few minutes, but only its terminals: the browser page, editor and diff tabs stay exactly as they were, and the terminals replay their screen when you switch back.
