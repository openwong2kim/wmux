### Fixed

- **A pane's identity is checked against its live shell right after a
  restart too.** For a couple of seconds after a background-service restart,
  or after a supervised pane restarted its process, wmux could still accept an
  old process ID as a pane's shell, so a process that had been given that ID
  could act as the pane. wmux now asks for the current list of shells whenever
  it hands out a pane's identity, and never reuses a list from before a
  restart. (#2323)
