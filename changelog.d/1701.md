### Added

- **The phone can read your channels.** A paired phone can now list the
  channels you can see, page through their messages, mark them read, and take
  your seat in one. The phone and the desktop share the same seat, so a channel
  you read on one surface is read on the other. A message that mentions you
  arrives on the phone's event stream as `channel.mention`. The daemon
  advertises `channels: true` in `/api/config` so the app knows the feature is
  there. You still cannot post from the phone.
