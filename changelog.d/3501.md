### Added

- **See what a headless worker has done between its replies.** For a fan-out task on a headless box, the box now reports at each of its agent's turn ends: the worktree's HEAD, when it was committed, and how many tracked files differ from where the task started. The fan-out poll returns these as `progress`, and `channel_mission_list` shows them, with the PC and branch, under the task's `remote`. A box on an older wmux sends none. (#3501)
