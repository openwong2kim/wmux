### Added

- **Moa reads the repositories it handed work to without asking.** When Moa checks an agent's result, Read, Grep and Glob inside that agent's repository no longer stop on a permission prompt. This lasts while the work is open and up to an hour after it ends, until Moa finishes the job. Secret files, `.git`, linked files and anything outside still ask, as do all edits and commands. Turn it off in Settings › Moa.
