### Changed

- **Automated prompts wait while a dialog or selection menu is on screen.**
  Scheduled prompts, automation runs and the fan-out caller nudge now check
  the pane for a dialog or a selection menu before they type, and again
  before they press Enter, the way they already checked for a folder-trust
  prompt. While one is up they wait and try again later; once it is gone the
  prompt is sent. Before, they were typed and submitted whenever the agent
  read as ready for input. A prompt that starts with a numbered list item is
  not mistaken for a menu. (#3299)
- **The automatic usage-limit continue waits while a selection menu is on
  the pane's screen.** That includes the agent's own usage-limit menu: if it
  is still showing when the limit resets, the continue waits until the menu
  is dismissed, then sends on its next check. Continue in wmux also waits
  while such a menu is up. Before, the continue was typed and submitted with
  the menu on screen. (#3299)
