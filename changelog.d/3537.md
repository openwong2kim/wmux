### Added

- `wmux setup-hooks --agent jcode` adds wmux's command to jcode's `[hooks]` in `~/.jcode/config.toml` (or `$JCODE_HOME/config.toml`), next to your own hooks and keeping your comments. `--status` checks it and `--remove` takes only wmux's commands out. When the file can't be edited safely, wmux changes nothing and prints the lines to add (#3067).
