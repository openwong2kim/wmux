### Fixed

- **Browser calls on an in-app tab no longer stall for about 4 seconds after
  a quiet spell (Windows).** When an in-app tab existed before the agent's
  browser connection was made, the first call after more than 10 seconds of
  quiet re-ran a slow page search that could never succeed there. That
  outcome is now remembered for the tab, so those calls take about as long
  as any other.
