### Added

- **Clear out your workspace's A2A tasks in one call.** `a2a_task_cancel` also takes a list of task ids, or a state with a minimum age in days (`state` + `older_than_days`), and `dry_run` lists what would be canceled without changing anything. The same works from a shell with `wmux task cancel <id…>` or `wmux task cancel --state submitted --older-than-days 7 --dry-run`. Only tasks your workspace sent or received are touched; another workspace's task is refused as before, and each cancel is recorded in the task log as your workspace. (#3068)
- **Optional retention for tasks that never started.** Settings → Roles & fan-out → "Close tasks that never started" takes a number of days (off by default). Once a day count is set, tasks a workspace sent that are still waiting to start after that long are closed as canceled, checked every hour, with one line per sweep in the daemon log. (#3068)
