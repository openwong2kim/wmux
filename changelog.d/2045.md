### Added

- **Edit a pane's browser protection from its menu.** A pane with its own
  Chrome profile gets "Browser protection…" in its ⋮ menu: turn protection on,
  choose any site or only the sites you list, and list sites to block. A
  protected pane's browser tabs carry a small muted lock. If the pane's profile
  or workspace changed since you confirmed its sites, the editor says so and
  saving confirms them again. Panes without a bound profile show the row
  disabled with what to do first; nothing else changes for them.
