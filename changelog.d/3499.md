### Added

- **WSL sign-in and status in Settings › Accounts.** On Windows, each Claude account now shows one line for each WSL distribution where its panes have reported. The line says whether the account is signed in there and shows its 5-hour and weekly usage. A signed-out line has its own **Sign in**. The account menu gains **Sign in for WSL** for each of those distributions and for the default one. These open a WSL tab that signs the account in inside that distribution. Each asks first, because opening the tab starts a distribution that is not running. (#3499)
