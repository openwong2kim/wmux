### Removed

- **The experimental `WMUX_RUST_SNAPSHOT=shadow` comparison mode.** It ran
  xterm and the native snapshot side by side for every eligible request. A
  daemon started with `WMUX_RUST_SNAPSHOT=shadow` now uses the default
  snapshot backend for its terminal engine; `text`, `ansi`, `all` and `off`
  are unchanged. (#3304)
