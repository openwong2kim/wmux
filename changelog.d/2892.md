### Added

- **Make a company in one pick from a preset.** The + menu now has a
  Company section with two presets: a researcher with three developers, and
  a lead with a builder and a reviewer. The dialog lets you change the name,
  folder, mission, each seat's name, what it owns and its agent, and which
  seat is CEO. Make then creates the whole company at once: a pane for each
  seat, named and started with its agent. Each agent's first turn tells it
  its seat, who its CEO is and how work is handed out, so the CEO can start
  sending briefs right away. A name that is already taken gets a `-2`
  suffix. Before, each of these steps was done by hand. (#2892)
