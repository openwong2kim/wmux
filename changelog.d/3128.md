### Changed

- **`wmux doctor` shows how often the native terminal engine rebuilds a screen and why it sometimes doesn't.** The memory section now has one line each for screen and text snapshots: how many were answered by the Rust engine, how many fell back to the slower JavaScript parser, and the reasons (for example a control sequence the engines read differently, or history past the engine's limit). This tells us which gap to close next. (#3128)
