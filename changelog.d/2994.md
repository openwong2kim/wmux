### Fixed

- **An A2A message to an idle pane is submitted, not left sitting in the
  prompt.** On a hidden pane, a message from another PC (single-line or
  multi-line) could be pasted into the agent's prompt and never submitted,
  while the sender saw it as delivered. Now, when wmux cannot confirm the pane
  is safe before pressing Enter, it checks once more; if it still cannot tell,
  it takes the text back out and delivers it again later. After Enter it
  watches for the submit and presses Enter once more if the prompt did not
  take it. A message that does stay in the prompt is held for you to check,
  and the sender is told why, instead of being counted as delivered.
