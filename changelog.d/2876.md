### Added

- **Company mode groundwork: departments and department tasks.** A workspace
  can belong to a department with one coordinator pane. A new task to a
  department pane must name that pane and carry a brief (goal, scope,
  acceptance, how to verify, what to report), and work from outside the
  department goes to its coordinator; a department task is canceled only by
  the pane that created it. There is no way to make a department in the app
  yet. (#2876)
