### Changed

- **Outside tools connected through the browser relay are disconnected when
  what they were allowed to connect on goes away.** Before, a connected tool
  stayed connected until it or its Chrome went away. Now wmux disconnects it
  (and drops its unused relay tokens) when the protected-browser policy
  changes, when its workspace closes, and, for a script in a pane, when that
  pane closes or moves to another workspace, when the user stops or takes
  over a browser tab in its workspace, or when the pane's claim is revoked or
  issued anew. (#3477)
