### Changed

- **Outside tools connected through the browser relay are disconnected when
  what they were allowed to connect on goes away.** Before, a connected tool
  stayed connected until it or its Chrome went away. Now wmux disconnects it,
  and drops its unused relay tokens, when the protected-browser policy changes
  or when its workspace closes. A relay token being handed out while one of
  these happens is withdrawn, and the caller asks again. (#3477)
