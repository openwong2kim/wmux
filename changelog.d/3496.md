### Added

- **Fan-out workers can spread over your Claude accounts.** A new switch in
  Settings › Roles & fan-out, off by default, starts each Claude fan-out
  worker on the account of its group with the most quota left and the
  fewest workers. Before, every worker ran on the default login. Only
  accounts signed in within their own folder take part, so an account that
  signs in only with a saved token, or only inside WSL, is left out. The
  usage reserve holds a fan-out only when it holds every account in the
  pool, and a resumed worker returns to the account it ran on. Needs the
  wmux daemon.
