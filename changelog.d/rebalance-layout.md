### Added

- **Balance layout.** A button in the titlebar (at the right end, beside the window controls), the
  prefix key `=` and a command-palette entry give every column and row of the
  workspace an equal share again, however you had dragged the dividers. Seven
  side-by-side columns become one seventh of the window each, and a column split
  into rows divides its height evenly. You can also bind a key to it in
  Settings → Shortcuts.
