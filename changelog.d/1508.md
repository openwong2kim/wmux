### Changed

- **The sidebar sorts by what needs you.** Workspaces are now ordered by
  attention by default: needs you, then finished, running, unconfirmed and
  idle, the most recent first within each. Rows never move while the pointer
  is on the list — the new order applies after a short pause or when the
  pointer leaves — and a workspace you just created stays on top for a few
  minutes. Pin a workspace (right-click › Pin position) to keep it where it
  is. If you chose Manual or Recent activity in Settings, that choice is
  kept.

### Added

- **"Changed since you last looked" dot.** A small white dot on a workspace
  row and its agent row says an agent finished or started waiting for you
  while that workspace was out of view. It clears as soon as you look.
