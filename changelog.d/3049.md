### Fixed

- **A pane restored after a restart keeps its credentials.** Terminals you
  open get your environment variables named like a secret (`*_TOKEN`,
  `*_KEY`, ...), but wmux never saves those values to disk, so a pane brought
  back after an upgrade or reboot came back without them and tools in it
  reported missing auth. A restored pane now gets them again from the running
  app, and they are still never written to disk. (#3049)
