### Added

- With "Spread fan-out workers over accounts" on and WSL as the default shell, fan-out workers now spread over the pool accounts' own homes in that distro. The pool picks by each home's remaining usage, then by how few workers already run on it. This works even when those accounts are signed in only inside WSL. A home that is out of usage, held by the usage reserve, or not signed in there is skipped. With none left, workers run on the calling pane's account as before.
