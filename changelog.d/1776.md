### Added

- **Moa remembers precedents and skills you approve.** When you answer one of Moa's questions, or Moa drafts a skill, a "Remember this?" card asks you to Save or Discard. A saved answer becomes a precedent in Moa's memory, and a saved skill becomes a Claude Code skill in Moa's brain folder. Moa reads both at the start of a new conversation as background, not as instructions. Moa cannot write its memory itself: it may only drop proposal files in one folder, and nothing is kept until you click Save. Settings → Moa lists what is kept, with Remove, and a switch to stop the proposals. With Moa off, nothing is proposed. (#1776)
