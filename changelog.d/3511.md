### Changed

- A restored terminal on a very wide pane keeps fewer history rows. Every snapshot of a pane's history (a reattach, a reload, a screen read) now holds at most what the native terminal core fits in four million cells: about 20,000 rows at 200 columns and 4,000 at 1,000 columns, even when the scrollback setting is higher. In return, such a pane is restored by the native core instead of a second, slower parse. A pane under 40 columns keeps the full 100,000 rows.
