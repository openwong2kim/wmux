### Fixed

- **The phone no longer sees Stop advertised when the server cannot serve
  it.** On a `wmux web` started without `--allow-transcript`, `/api/config`
  still told the phone it could stop a running turn and queue a message.
  Every Stop, and every queued send, then failed with "transcript-disabled".
  The chat write features are now advertised only to a caller their routes
  would accept. (#1672)
