### Changed

- **An A2A message a hidden pane could not take is kept and typed later.**
  When wmux cannot confirm that a hidden agent pane is free of a folder-trust
  prompt, or the pane is held by a usage limit, a local task's message is no
  longer simply refused: wmux keeps it and types it when the pane's agent ends
  a turn, or on a check once a minute, through the same checks as a new send.
  A later message on the same task waits behind it. The sender's receipt says
  so (`storedForLater`). The kept copy is dropped after 24 hours, when the
  task ends, when the pane closes or its agent changes, or when more than 8
  messages wait for the pane; the task stays readable with `a2a_task_query`.
